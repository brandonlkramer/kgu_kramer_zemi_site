Weeks 5–12 · from your research log to a work-in-progress research proposal

Week by week Main deadlines

The rest of this semester

Every week, you read one new paper and complete a Worksheet 5A sheet. Starting this week, you also keep a research log, where all your reading builds up. By Week 12, you will have a literature review draft, a methods draft and a revised introduction: a work-in-progress research proposal that you will continue in the spring of your 4th year.

毎週、論文を1本読み、ワークシート5Aを書きます。今週からは、読んだ論文をすべてリサーチログに記録していきます。第12週までに、文献レビューの下書き、研究方法の下書き、修正した序論をまとめ、研究計画書の途中版として提出します。4年生の春学期に続けて仕上げていきます。

5Set up your research logWhat a research log is for. Create your Google Sheets file and enter one paper together.Enter every paper you have read so far in the Reading log tab and tag each one by topic. 5A for one new article.
6Methods and participantsDesign, instrument, procedure, participants: find each of these in a paper you know.Fill the Methods and Participants tabs for your most relevant papers. 5A for one new article.
7Connecting studies and groupingHow to connect two studies (similarly, in contrast, together these suggest). Filter your log by topic tag to find groups.One connecting paragraph. Literature review outline v1: section headings and the studies under each. 5A for one new article.
8The upside-down funnelFrom general to specific: analyse a published literature review layer by layer.Put your outline in funnel order and mark the gap. Research question draft.
9Writing a literature review sectionTurn 5A paragraphs and connecting paragraphs into one section.Draft your first literature review section. 5A for one new article.
10Choosing a methodSort your Methods tab by design: how did other researchers study questions like yours? Who can you realistically recruit?Method plan: participants, instruments, procedure, analysis (bullet points).
11Peer reviewExchange literature review drafts. Consent and permission for collecting data.Revise your literature review draft. Write a methods draft from your method plan.
12Taking stockDoes your introduction lead to the gap, the gap to your question, and your question to your method? Plan for the 4th-year spring.Submit your work-in-progress proposal: revised introduction, literature review draft, methods draft.
