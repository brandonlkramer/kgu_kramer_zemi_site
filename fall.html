Fall schedule

Weeks 5–12 · from your research log to final drafts of your proposal

Week by week Main deadlines

The rest of this semester

Every week, you read two to three new papers and complete a Worksheet 5A sheet for each one. Starting this week, you also keep a research log, where all your reading builds up. By Week 12, you will submit final drafts of your introduction, literature review and methods, which you will continue to develop in the spring of your 4th year.

毎週、論文を2〜3本読み、それぞれについてワークシート5Aを書きます。今週からは、読んだ論文をすべてリサーチログに記録していきます。第12週には、序論・文献レビュー・研究方法の最終稿を提出し、4年生の春学期にさらに発展させていきます。
