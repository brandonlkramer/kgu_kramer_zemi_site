Research log

One Google Sheets file with three tabs: everything you read, how each study was done, and who took part

Set up Tab 1: Reading log Tab 2: Methods Tab 3: Participants How you will use it

Set up

Create one Google Sheets file in your folder in the class Google Drive. Name it Research log – [your name] and make three tabs: Reading log, Methods and Participants.

クラスのGoogleドライブの自分のフォルダに、Googleスプレッドシートを1つ作成します。ファイル名は「Research log – 名前」とし、Reading log、Methods、Participantsの3つのタブを作ってください。

Every paper gets a number in the Reading log tab. Use the same number on its Worksheet 5A sheet and in the other two tabs, so you can always find everything about one paper.

You start the log by entering every paper you have read so far. After that, add each new paper in the same week you read it.

One row for every paper you read.

Finding the instrument: look for an appendix, the source the authors cite for the questionnaire or test, or the supplementary materials on the journal website.

3Participants

One row for every paper that is relevant to your own study.

No.WhoNumberAge, year or levelContextRecruitment
Same as the Reading logLearner type, e.g. first-year English majorsTotal, and per group if there were groupsWhatever the paper reportsCountry, type of institution, type of classHow participants were chosen, e.g. the author's own classes, volunteers

How you will use it

  • Literature review outline: filter the Reading log by topic tag to see which studies belong together.
  • Choosing a method: sort the Methods tab by design to see how other researchers studied questions like yours, and which instruments appear more than once.
  • Planning participants: the Participants tab shows how many participants similar studies had and how they were recruited.
