The year in brief

Every week in both semesters, you read one paper and complete a Worksheet 5A sheet. In Spring, you build your reading skills and write a first introduction. In Fall, you turn your summaries into a literature review, design your methods, and finish the year with a research proposal: introduction, literature review and methods.

両学期とも、毎週論文を1本読み、ワークシート5Aを書きます。春学期は読む力をつけ、序論の最初の版を書きます。秋学期は要約を文献レビューにまとめ、研究方法を決めて、年度末に研究計画書（序論・文献レビュー・研究方法）を提出します。

Spring

Reading moves from easy to hard: abstracts, then Japanese full papers and one-page OASIS summaries, then one full English article.

Fall

Keep reading one new article a week. Your research log becomes the place where your work builds up.
