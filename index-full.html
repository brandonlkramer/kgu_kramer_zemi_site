How this zemi works

Over four semesters, you will plan, carry out and write up your own research project in English. The work is divided into milestones. Each milestone has one clear output, so you can always see what to do next.

4学期をかけて、自分の研究を計画・実施し、英語で論文にまとめます。作業はマイルストーンに分かれていて、それぞれに提出物があります。次に何をすればいいか、いつでも確認できます。

If you miss a class, check the milestone list below, find your next step, and keep working. Please read the ground rules before the first class.
